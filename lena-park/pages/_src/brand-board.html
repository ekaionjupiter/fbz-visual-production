The emblem is a thin crescent inside an open circle, a nightlight left on in a dark room. The circle opens at both sides, so the mark feels like a hush rather than a badge.

Left: the AI-generated concept from Higgsfield. Top: the vector redraw used in production. Minimum size for the emblem is 20px. Clear space equals the height of the crescent on every side.
