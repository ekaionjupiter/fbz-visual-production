## Rhythm and spacing extracted
- Content column is about 820 to 900px inside a 1440 frame. Text blocks are about 560 to 600px wide.
- Section padding is about 100 to 140px at desktop. Sections alternate dark and light, with a photo-backed band every 3 to 4 sections.
- There is a CTA after almost every section (about 14 on the page). The button style is always the same: one color, one label.
- Bordered boxes are 1px with a warm accent color, square-ish corners and about 16px of internal padding.
- Pillar cards are dark-on-dark, with a large ghost numeral (about 90px) top-right at about 10% opacity.
- Split sections run their image full-bleed to the viewport edge on one half.

## Deliberate departures (brand, not quality)
- No all-caps condensed shouting. Fraunces and DM Sans at calm weights.
- No "limited spots", guarantee-of-outcome or fear-based "two futures" content (DO NOT CLAIM list).
- Rounded corners (14 to 32px) instead of square boxes, to keep it soft.
- Fewer CTAs per page (about 7) and calmer intervals, which matches "fewer decisions after dark".
