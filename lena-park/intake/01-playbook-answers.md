## Section 1: You and Your Work

**1. In one sentence, what do you help people do?**
I help exhausted first-time parents build calm, predictable nights with their baby in the first months, without cry-it-out pressure and without guessing.

**2. Who do you help? Be specific.**
First-time parents of babies from birth to about 6 months. Mostly moms between 28 and 38, often returning to work around month 3 or 4, usually with a partner who wants to help but doesn't know how. Many are high-functioning professionals who are used to being good at things and feel completely lost at 3 a.m.

**3. What's your background? Why should people trust you?**
I have a PhD in developmental psychology. My dissertation work was on infant routines and parental stress. After that I spent several years coordinating research in a university infant development lab. I trained and certified as a pediatric sleep consultant in 2019 and have run a private 1:1 practice since 2020. I am not a medical doctor and I'm careful to say so. I'm a mother of two. My first was a difficult sleeper and that's honestly how I ended up here.

**4. What results have your clients gotten?**
I try not to promise outcomes because every baby is different. What I hear most often:
- Parents say they finally feel like they know what to do in the evening instead of improvising.
- Partners start sharing nights because there's a plan both of them understand.
- Parents stop googling at 2 a.m.
- A lot of them say "I feel like myself again" at the end of our time together.
I have written testimonials from about 60 families. I'd want to only use ones where I have explicit permission.

**5. How do you currently sell your work?**
1:1 packages only. A 3-week support package at $650 and a 6-week package at $1,100. I'm booked about 7 weeks out right now and turning people away every week. Clients come from Instagram and referrals from two local pediatric practices and a lactation consultant I trust.

**6. Audience size**
- Instagram: about 8,000 followers (@quiethours.sleep)
- Email list: about 1,200 subscribers, mostly from a free PDF I made years ago ("Bedtime Basics") that I think looks dated now
- I post 3 to 4 times a week. Carousels do best. Reels feel uncomfortable for me but I do them.

## Section 2: The Product You Want to Build

**7. What product do you want to launch first?**
A self-paced mini-course called "First 90 Nights." It covers the foundations I teach every 1:1 family in the first two sessions: rhythm, the sleep space, how to respond at night, and how to share the load with a partner. I keep repeating the same foundations on every call. Families who get those foundations early do so much better.

**8. Who is it for and who is it NOT for?**
For: first-time parents, pregnant in the third trimester or with a baby under about 4 months, who want a calm plan before things fall apart.
Not for: families with medical concerns about their baby's sleep or breathing (they need their pediatrician), parents looking for a strict sleep training protocol for an older baby, anyone wanting a guaranteed "sleep through the night by week X" promise.

**9. What price are you thinking?**
I honestly don't know. My 1:1 work starts at $650. I don't want it to feel cheap but I want new parents to be able to say yes without a big discussion. Somewhere between $97 and $197?

**10. What's your biggest fear about launching?**
That it will feel like every other loud sleep program on Instagram. I don't want countdown timers screaming at tired people. And I'm worried about saying something that sounds like medical advice.

**11. What's your timeline?**
I'd like a waitlist open by mid-October and doors open in early November so families have it before the holidays.

## Section 3: Brand and Voice

**12. Three words that describe how you want people to feel?**
Calm. Capable. Accompanied.

**13. Brands or people whose style you admire?**
Aesop for the restraint. The way some midwives talk, very steady. I like the old Kinfolk magazine look. I do not want anything that looks like a baby store: no primary colors, no cartoon clouds, no pastel rainbow.

**14. Words or phrases you use a lot?**
"Let's make tonight smaller." "You are not doing it wrong." "Rhythm, not schedule." "Good enough is the goal at 3 a.m." "Your baby is not a problem to solve." "One small change at a time."

**15. Words you never want associated with you?**
"Hack." "Sleep training boot camp." "Guaranteed." "Fix your baby." "Drowsy but awake" used as a magic rule. "Self-soothe" used as a pass or fail test. Anything shaming.

**16. Do you already have a brand name, logo or colors?**
My Instagram is @quiethours.sleep and I've loosely called the practice "Quiet Hours" for two years, but there's no real logo. I'd love to make that official: Quiet Hours, with my name attached. My current colors are random. I like deep evening blue, warm cream and something like lamplight.
