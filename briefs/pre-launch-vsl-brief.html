Video Brief: The Pre-Launch VSL

Freedom Builderz · Plays on the OTO page (Option 2) and on the Pre-Launch sales page · Mike on camera · 3:00 max · Version 2, matched to the Pre-Launch Sales Page and Playbook OTO copy · October 1, 2026 · Prepared by Kai Stone

1. What this video is

A talking-head sales video from Mike that plays right after someone buys a low-ticket product (the Expert Freedom Playbook or the Marketing Course). On the one-time offer page they see two doors: Option 1 is the Strategic Action Plan plus the 2-hour workshop at $297, Option 2 is the Pre-Launch at $2,000. This video sells Option 2. The same video drops into the "Mike walks you through the Pre-Launch" player on the Pre-Launch sales page, so every line below is written to agree with that page word for word.

Who is watching: an expert, coach or consultant who just paid $17 to $97, knows the product hiding in their expertise, and is now asking "how do I get people to buy it." Warm, a little trusting, one click from leaving.

What the video has to do: show the finished Pre-Launch so they can see what they would own, make Option 2 feel like the obvious next door, and get the click on "Start My Pre-Launch." It does not explain the whole business. It makes the kit real.

2. The offer the video sells (copied from the sales page)

The Freedom Builderz Pre-Launch: $2,000, or 2 x $1,000, financing available. Up to 50 clients a month. Built for you and delivered in 48 to 72 hours after the call with Mike. Every dollar credits toward the $10K or $18K full build if they upgrade within 30 days of delivery. Option 2 on the sales page adds the Official Brand Board for $500 more ($2,500).

The ten things the page says we build, in the page's order (the video names each one):

  1. Full program landing page plus thank-you page (launches as the waitlist page, converts into the sales page later)
  2. Starter brand board (colors, type, a usable mark)
  3. Waitlist opt-in with exit popup
  4. Waitlist bonuses (founding member rate, a one on one call for the first 20, the free quick-win PDF)
  5. Quick-win PDF, written and designed
  6. $17 audit PDF, written and designed (their first paid product)
  7. Custom offer cart for the $17 audit, in their brand
  8. 3 to 4 waitlist emails, written and set up
  9. 3 post-purchase emails, automated
  10. Traffic kit (5 Canva ad templates, a branding video, a Business Manager installer guide, a $10 to $20 a day budget plan)

How it starts (the page's five steps): buy and open the Builderz Portal, open Kajabi through the partner link (3 months for $100), complete the questionnaires (about 30 minutes), one call with Mike to lock the direction, then the build clock starts. Delivery: one Google Drive folder and one Figma file with every page as editable frames, emailed to them, ready to drop into Kajabi or GoHighLevel.

What Mike must not say on camera: any income number, any guarantee, "results typical," or a promise of sales. The page's milestone ("first 3 to 10 audit sales, waitlist signups, booked calls") can be named as what the Pre-Launch is built to go after, never as what will happen.

3. Why this script works

  • It picks up exactly where the OTO page leaves off. The viewer was just offered a plan ($297). The script honors that door and then opens the bigger one: "or we build the proof of demand for you." No contradiction, no confusion between the two options.
  • It repeats the sales page's core argument in Mike's mouth. "Most experts build first and launch to no one. The experts who win do it backwards." Same idea, same words as the letter on the page, so the video and the page reinforce each other instead of competing.
  • It shows instead of tells. About half the runtime is screen footage of a finished kit in the page's exact deliverable order. People buy what they can see.
  • It removes the two objections that stop a $2,000 click. "I will have to do the work" (no, built for you in 48 to 72 hours) and "what if I go further" (every dollar credits toward the build within 30 days).
  • It is calm. No countdown, no shouting. The confidence is in the footage, and the only scarcity line is the one already on the page: up to 50 clients a month.

Because nobody was waiting for it. The experts who win do it backwards. They prove demand first, and they build second.

Forty-eight to seventy-two hours later it lands in your inbox: one Google Drive folder and one Figma file where every page is editable, ready to drop into Kajabi or GoHighLevel.

[2:05 to 2:30 · Mike to camera, the pricing line and the credit line appear on screen]

The Pre-Launch is two thousand dollars, or two payments of one thousand, and financing is available. And here is the part I want you to hear: if you have us build the whole program later, every dollar of this counts toward the build. You are putting two thousand dollars down on proof.

We take up to fifty of these a month, because the team builds every one by hand. This is step two. If you want your first buyers waiting before you build, pick Option 2 below this video. I will see you on the call.

Style rules

  • Overlay fonts and colors come from the Playbook sales page system (dark surface #0A0A0F, cream, Playbook orange CTA). One font family for captions.
  • Captions in sentence case, max two lines, no emojis. No em or en dashes.
  • Music under the voice at all times; nothing that swells into launch hype.
  • No stock footage of people, no AI-looking visuals. The product footage is the proof.
  • Nothing on screen may show or promise results, revenue, or sales numbers. Keep the earnings disclaimer in the page footer, not in the video.

6. Recording notes for Mike

  • Read it as a conversation. Shorten any sentence that does not sound like you, but keep the numbers exactly as written; they match the page.
  • Record 0:50 to 1:40 as a separate voice-over while you scroll the Figma file yourself; it sounds more natural than narrating from memory.
  • Two takes of the open and the close. The open is where people decide.
  • The sales page player currently says "Six minutes." Since this cut is under three, that line on the page changes to "Three minutes" when the video goes live.
  • Option 2 on the sales page (the Official Brand Board add-on at $2,500) is not mentioned in the video on purpose; the page sells that upgrade on its own.
